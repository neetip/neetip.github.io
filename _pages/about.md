I am computer scientist with 10+ years of research and research leadership at the intersection of trustworthy AI, national security, and public policy across government, academia, and non-profits. Currently, I am the Director, Office of AI Innovation and Integration, at University at Buffalo, NY, where I lead efforts to develop, drive and support AI efforts across the university to aid in responsible adoption. I also hold an adjunct researcher position at RAND. When full-time at RAND, I lead studies and analytical work on policy relevant questions related to developing, deploying, and operationalizing AI for challenging contexts in national security. I am also leading work on developing AI models for better understanding societal well-being. I am a member of the Association for Computing Machinery’s U.S. Tech Policy Committee and contribute to its policy pieces. I am a visiting scholar at the Department of Computer Science, Dartmouth College. 

Before RAND, I worked at the National Science Foundation, where I was the 2022-23 AAAS Science and Technology Policy fellow at the National AI Research Institutes program and in the leadership team of the ExpandAI program. I was the 2022 Mirzayan Policy Fellow at the National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine, where I contributed to the work of the Committee on National Statistics on aspects of AI governance.

I have completed my postdoc from the Department of Computer Science at Dartmouth College and my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the State University of New York at Buffalo, where my doctoral work was awarded the Chih Foundation Research Award. Before my Ph.D., I was a researcher at Oak Ridge National Laboratory. I obtained my M.S. in Computer Science from the University of California, Riverside, where I received the Dean’s Distinguished Fellowship.
